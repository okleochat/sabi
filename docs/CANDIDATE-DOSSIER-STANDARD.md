# Candidate dossier evidence standard

**Snapshot checked:** 27 November 2026
**Roster source:** INEC updated final presidential list, 31 October 2026

## What each dossier contains

Each of the 18 named INEC presidential tickets has a profile containing the presidential candidate and running mate, official particulars as filed with INEC, original source links, background/policy/public-record sections, a public campaign-evidence snapshot, manifesto-document status and a common set of questions for public accountability.

INEC-listed age, gender and qualifications are reproduced as filed. They are not independent certificate verification. Where a biography, platform, speech or document has not been independently sourced, the page marks that gap rather than inventing a career history or policy position.

## Third-party campaign monitor

Sabi uses the OpenPolls Africa Nigeria 2027 campaign tracker as a discovery source for documented campaign events and public finance disclosures. The 27 November 2026 snapshot includes a reviewed-record count for each of the 18 named presidential candidates; most profiles also show documented campaign-event counts and whether the tracker located finance records.

These counts are source-discovery figures. They measure what that tracker reviewed or found in its monitored sources, not the total amount of campaigning, campaign strength, public support, popularity, candidate merit or activity outside the tracker. A short record list is not evidence a campaign is inactive; a long list is not evidence of support. A zero finance-record count means no record was found by that tracker in the checked snapshot, not that no finance disclosure or spending exists.

Source: https://openpolls.africa/2027

## Manifesto catalogue

The OpenPolls Africa manifesto catalogue was checked separately. It identifies an NDP 2027 manifesto with extracted first-year commitments on industrial clusters and SME support, export support and port trade facilitation, tax administration/expenditure/procurement reform, and agricultural/SME credit. It reports several other campaigns as having announced or launched a manifesto without the original full document being located. One SDP entry is internally inconsistent: it labels the manifesto as published but its note says the party intended to publish a policy blueprint later. Sabi preserves this uncertainty.

Where no manifesto document is shown in the source snapshot, the dossier says that the source did not locate one; it does not claim the candidate has made no promises. A candidate's campaign speech or policy interview is not silently substituted for the original manifesto.

Source: https://openpolls.africa/manifestos

## Current limitations

- Some ticket profiles have independently sourced biographical summaries and licensed portraits; others still need individual sources and portrait permissions.
- The campaign tracker is third-party and its extraction coverage depends on publicly readable sources and press coverage. Its counts are not comparable as a measure of total candidate activity.
- The INEC list includes 18 named presidential tickets. A separate court-order entry with no candidate name is not turned into an invented 19th person.
- No candidate gets a numerical score, ranking, endorsement or predicted chance of winning from this dossier system.

## Editorial maintenance

Update the roster only from INEC notices or court orders. Preserve source publication dates and the event dates separately. Add a biography or manifesto claim only when the original source or reliable corroboration is linked. When a source changes its record, preserve the older value and log the correction date.
