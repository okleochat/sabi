## 1A. Initial state-impact benchmark and promise-ledger references

### 2025 pSPI state-performance benchmark

Sabi now displays a rank-normalised external benchmark for each of the 36 governor profiles. The 2025 Phillips Consulting State Performance Index covered 37 indicators and combined 70% objective data with 30% citizen-perception data across health, education, infrastructure, governance, economic development and digital access. The 2025 ranking list used for the benchmark is: Lagos 1, Ogun 2, Kaduna 3, Adamawa 4, Niger 5, Nasarawa 6, Gombe 7, Jigawa 8, Plateau 9, Abia 10, Oyo 11, Bauchi 12, Ebonyi 13, Cross River 14, Kebbi 15, Edo 16, Delta 17, Kwara 18, Yobe 19, Enugu 20, Akwa Ibom 21, Osun 22, Benue 23, Taraba 24, Rivers 25, Ekiti 26, Ondo 27, Zamfara 28, Bayelsa 29, Kano 30, Imo 31, Katsina 32, Sokoto 33, Anambra 34, Kogi 35 and Borno 36.

Sabi converts the published ordinal rank into a 0–100 percentile using `(36 - rank) / 35 × 100`, rounding to the nearest integer. The page shows both the percentile and original rank. **This is a rank-normalised external benchmark, not Phillips Consulting's raw score and not Sabi's own five-dimension Impact Score.** It is a cross-state snapshot, not proof that the incumbent personally caused the outcome. The 2025 edition is used because it is the multidimensional 37-indicator edition; the revised 2026 pSPI is a distinct assessment with updated FY2025 financial data and different current-snapshot/trajectory measures, so its results must not be mixed into the 2025 rank series.

Sources:
- pSPI report hub: https://pspi.com.ng/pspi-report/
- 2025 rank list: https://wikkitimes.com/lagos-ogun-kaduna-top-2025-state-performance-rankings-pspi-report/
- 2025 methodology summary (37 KPIs; 70:30 objective/perception): https://punchng.com/pcl-unveils-2025-state-performance-index-a-scorecard-for-governance-and-development-in-nigeria/
- Revised 2026 methodology and FY2025 audited-account update: https://punchng.com/phillips-consulting-pcl-releases-revised-2026-state-performance-indexpspi-strengthening-the-benchmark-for-measuring-state-performance-in-nigeria/

### External promise-fulfilment references

For eight governors, Sabi displays a clearly attributed reference to The Receipt's publicly available manifesto-ledger figure, last updated 23 November 2026: Abia 75% (2 audited promises), Delta 37.5% (4), Enugu 70% (5), Kaduna 62.5% (4), Kano 30% (5), Lagos 60% (5), Oyo 62.5% (4) and Rivers 50% (4). The Receipt's methodology says it scores kept promises as 1, in-progress promises as 0.5 where physical or budgetary evidence exists, and broken/abandoned promises as 0. Its public ledgers have limited samples and may not cover a full manifesto; some state pages remain marked audit-in-progress.

Sources:

### Sabi score publication rule update

The 60% manifesto-catalogue threshold is now a **completeness label**, not a blanket publication blocker. If at least one commitment has approved, assessable evidence, the engine can publish a provisional score with its assessed sample and coverage caveat. Reaching 60% of identifiable commitments upgrades it to a complete-register assessment. The full Sabi Impact Score still requires at least 3 of 5 dimensions and 60% of planned indicator weight. External pSPI percentiles and third-party promise-ledger references must never be relabelled as Sabi's own scores.

## 1. State fiscal and service-delivery evidence

### 2025 fiscal performance

BudgIT's *2026 State of States* report assesses the 2025 fiscal year across 35 states. Rivers State is excluded because the report says the state did not produce a 2025 audited financial statement or budget implementation reports. BudgIT says it used audited reports for revenue and expenditure, Q4 budget implementation reports for critical-sector spending, and Debt Management Office data at 31 December 2025 for debt comparisons.

- Report and methodology: https://budgit.org/post_publications/from-allocation-to-impact-rethinking-state-spending-priorities-2026-state-of-states-report/
- Full report: https://budgit.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/States26SMDF1.pdf
- Example fiscal observations: Lagos internally generated revenue was reported at ₦1.71tn; Enugu IGR increased from ₦180.5bn in 2024 to ₦407.76bn in 2025 (+125.91%).

**Interpretation rule:** fiscal capacity, IGR, debt and budget execution are not governor performance scores. They are inputs to fiscal analysis. They do not independently establish service quality, project completion or improved household outcomes. Rivers' missing reports are a data-availability and transparency gap, not proof of a positive or negative outcome.

### Health outcome signal: childhood vaccination

The 2024 State of Health of the Nation report, using the 2023–24 NDHS, reports full basic vaccination among children aged 12–23 months at 39% nationally, compared with 31% in 2018. Bayelsa increased from 18.0% to 63.4% (+45.4 percentage points). Gombe, Bauchi, Jigawa, Ogun and Katsina more than doubled their previous survey values. Sokoto increased from 4.6% to 8.4%, still below the national estimate. Eight states showed declines.

Source: https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/The-State-of-Health-of-the-Nation-Report-2024.pdf.

**Interpretation rule:** NDHS survey estimates and DHIS2 administrative coverage are different data series and must not be combined. Some mortality indicators use multi-year look-back periods and should not be attributed to a governor's short tenure.

**Status:** official ministry self-report; not an independently verified Sabi finding. Obtain the complete list of 41 commitments, the original baselines and deadlines, release-level financial records, facility-level verification and outcome data before assigning any commitment status.

### Electricity generation

NERC figures previously reviewed indicate average grid generation of about 3,929 MW in 2022 and 4,144 MW in 2024; October 2026 average hourly grid generation was reported at about 4,047 MW.

Sources: https://nerc.gov.ng/ and the relevant NERC quarterly reports.

**Interpretation rule:** these grid figures do not substantiate a claim of doubled grid generation on this measure. Do not conflate these measures.

## 3B. Independent promise-tracking methods reviewed

The Receipt publishes a separate governor promise-ledger and a public methodology page:
- Methodology: https://www.thereceipt.ng/methodology
- Governor directory: https://www.thereceipt.ng/
- Example Abia ledger: https://www.thereceipt.ng/governors/abia
- Example Borno ledger: https://www.thereceipt.ng/governors/borno
- Example Oyo ledger: https://www.thereceipt.ng/governors/oyo

Sabi does **not** adopt The Receipt's scores, rankings, labels or verdicts. Its methodology page is useful as a process reference: it says it tracks documented manifestos, inauguration addresses and formal early-term policy documents; keeps vague commitments visible but excludes them from its index; requires physical or budgetary evidence for an "in progress" state; treats press releases as claims rather than proof; separates citizen verdicts from editorial assessment; and offers a right-of-reply and public correction process.

The public directory reports 98 promises across 36 governors, while the example state pages expose only a small subset of each governor's commitments. This is not enough to establish that each full manifesto has been catalogued. Sabi's separate 60% manifesto-catalogue gate is intended to prevent any score from being presented as comprehensive when the promise register is only a small sample. Use this site only to discover original promises and evidence leads, then independently locate and verify the original manifesto and primary records.

## 4. Scoring readiness and evidence gaps

1. Do not publish a governor's Integrity Score until at least 60% of the identifiable commitments in that governor's original manifesto/pledge set have been catalogued. Show catalogue coverage separately from evidence coverage.
2. Do not publish an Impact Score until at least 3 of 5 dimensions and 60% of planned indicator weight are assessable.
3. Do not translate fiscal rankings, budget execution or ministry press releases into overall performance scores.
4. For every state, collect the original manifesto, budget and Q4 implementation report, audited accounts, procurement and project records, and comparable outcome series.
5. Rivers requires special source work because the cited BudgIT report excludes it for missing audited and implementation reports. Seek state records, Auditor-General reports, legislative records, procurement evidence and credible independent reporting; label unresolved gaps.
6. No politician's Integrity or Impact score has been calculated or changed by this evidence register.

## 5. Source-quality hierarchy

1. Original legal, INEC, court, budget, audited-account, regulator and official statistical records.
2. State and federal ministry / agency data, with underlying tables and dates.
3. Independent fiscal and sector research with published methods and downloadable datasets.
4. Credible reporting for context and cross-checking.
5. Campaign material and press releases as evidence of what was promised or claimed, not proof that delivery occurred.

## 0–10 category score release (checked 27 November 2026)

Category scores are provisional indicator scores. They are not averaged into a single politician rating. The exact formulas and limitations are published in `SABI-INTELLIGENCE-METHODOLOGY.md`.

### Fiscal revenue peer score
- 2025 state IGR values are from the NBS 2025 release and are ranked among the 36 states.
- The FCT is kept out of the 36-state governor comparison; the FCT Minister's FCT score is separately ranked against all 37 jurisdictions.
- Formula: 10 × (N − rank) ÷ (N − 1); highest total nominal IGR receives 10.0, lowest receives 0.0.
- 2024 comparison/reporting context: Joint Tax Board, 2024/2025 Year Book, page 14: https://cms-api.jrb.gov.ng/api/publication-assets/file/2024-2025-jtb-year-book.pdf
- This is total nominal revenue, not per-capita revenue, inflation-adjusted growth or a complete measure of prosperity. The peer position is a descriptive fiscal-scale score only.

### Education and health execution scores
- Education: BudgIT, 2026 State of States Report, page 155, FY2025 education implementation table: https://budgit.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/States26SMDF1.pdf
- Health: BudgIT, 2026 Health Financing Report, FY2025 health budget performance table: https://budgit.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/HealthFinancingReport__.pdf
- Formula for each budget execution indicator: max(0, 10 − |100 − execution percentage| ÷ 10), capped at 10.0.
- Exact 100% execution receives 10.0. Spending below or above the approved budget receives a lower score; overspending is not rewarded. Budget execution does not prove service quality, learning or health outcomes.
- Dataset omissions remain disclosed. The education report excludes Rivers; the health table excludes Akwa Ibom and Rivers. An excluded jurisdiction is not assigned zero.

### NDHS health outcome score
- Primary source: Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey 2024, published September 2025: https://www.health.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/NDHS-2024-Final-Report_Disseminated17102025.pdf
- Indicator: percentage of children aged 12–23 months fully vaccinated for all basic antigens.
- Formula: coverage percentage ÷ 10.
- Limitation: survey coverage level, not change during a specific governor's tenure or proof of a single officeholder's contribution. It is not interchangeable with routine administrative immunisation reporting.

### President's 2026 macro benchmark scores
- Actual values: Federal Ministry of Finance Nigeria Macro Dashboard, https://finance.gov.ng/?page_id=26
- Official 2026 assumptions: National Assembly document, https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11234 (real GDP growth projection 4.68%; inflation assumption 16.5%).
- GDP score formula: min(10, Q2 2026 real GDP growth ÷ 4.68 × 10).
- Inflation score formula: min(10, 16.5 ÷ October 2026 year-on-year headline inflation × 10).
- The category score is the arithmetic mean of these two benchmark scores.
- Limitation: the current readings are partial-year observations and do not match the full-year budget-assumption period exactly. This is provisional directional benchmark tracking, not a final annual score. Food inflation, external reserves and public debt remain contextual indicators and are not included in this score.

### Coverage and non-scored categories
- Each category shows its number of available indicators. Health coverage may be 1/2 in states omitted from the FY2025 health budget table.
- Integrity and manifesto delivery remain explicitly "Not scored yet" until the item-by-item commitment registers and approved evidence decisions are complete.
- Security and infrastructure remain unscored until comparable, source-validated indicators with appropriate baselines have been added.
- No missing category is assigned zero; no overall average is calculated across unlike categories.


### Overall Score rule (27 November 2026)
- Each officeholder profile now displays an Overall Score out of 10.
- Formula: arithmetic mean of all category scores currently published for that officeholder, with equal weight for each category; rounded to one decimal place.
- Categories marked "Not scored yet" are excluded from both the sum and the mean. They are never assigned zero.
- Every Overall Score displays scored categories / total applicable assessment categories and the resulting coverage percentage.
- Because the available categories and periods can differ between the President, governors and FCT Minister, the Overall Score is provisional and must not be used as an unqualified league table. Inspect the separate category scores, source dates and disclosed gaps.
